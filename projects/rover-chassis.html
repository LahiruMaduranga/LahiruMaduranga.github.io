Overview

A mobile robotics platform for autonomous-system development. The prototype carries a LiDAR sensor on top, a ZED Mini stereo camera and additional cameras at the front, and its control electronics on an off-road chassis with independent suspension.

Problem

Developing autonomous behaviour needs a dependable mobile base where sensing, control, and perception can be integrated and tested together.

Engineering objectives

  • Build a rigid, serviceable mobile base.
  • Integrate motor control with an embedded controller.
  • Mount and wire onboard sensors for perception work.
  • Provide a platform for autonomous-system development.
  • Detect AprilTag markers in the test area for visual reference.

System architecture

  1. LiDAR + stereo camera
  2. Perception computer
  3. Embedded controller
  4. Motor & steering control
  5. Off-road chassis

Simplified, illustrative architecture

Hardware & build

The photographed prototype uses an off-road RC chassis with oil-filled shocks, a stacked mounting frame with a LiDAR on top, a ZED Mini stereo camera and two further cameras at the front, and the control electronics and battery inside the frame. AprilTag markers placed on objects around the test area give the cameras known visual reference points.

Technical approach

An off-road RC chassis is fitted with a mounting frame for a top LiDAR, a forward ZED Mini stereo camera, extra cameras, and the onboard control electronics, so perception and control can be developed on real hardware.

Engineering considerations

  • Routing power and signal wiring so it survives vibration and stays serviceable.
  • Mounting sensors with known positions relative to the chassis.
  • Separating low-level motor control from higher-level perception.
