Overview

An automatic license plate recognition unit with gate control. A camera inside the standing enclosure captures approaching vehicles, and the recognition result drives the gate-control output.

Problem

Gate access that depends on manual checks is slow and inconsistent at busy entrances.

Engineering objectives

  • Recognize vehicle license plates from a fixed camera position.
  • Turn a recognition result into a gate-control decision.
  • House the electronics in a self-contained roadside unit.

System architecture

  1. Camera
  2. Plate detection
  3. Character recognition
  4. Access decision
  5. Gate controller

Simplified, illustrative architecture

Hardware & build

The unit shown is a standing enclosure with a front-facing imaging window and an integrated light.

Technical approach

An enclosed unit reads vehicle license plates with a camera and uses the result to drive a gate-control output.

Engineering considerations

  • Changing daylight, glare, and night-time illumination at the camera window.
  • Plate angle and distance as vehicles approach the gate.
  • Keeping gate behaviour safe when a plate cannot be read.
