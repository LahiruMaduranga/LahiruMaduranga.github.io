Overview

A computer-vision workflow for garment quality control: garments are captured with a camera and measured automatically. This is a confidential project, so technical details are kept at a high level.

Problem

Measuring garments by hand for quality control is slow and varies between operators.

Engineering objectives

  • Measure garments automatically from camera images.
  • Give consistent results across operators.
  • Fit into a simple capture-and-measure workflow.

System architecture

  1. Camera capture
  2. Computer vision
  3. Calibrated measurement
  4. Operator workflow

Simplified, illustrative architecture

Hardware & build

A camera capture setup photographs garments laid flat. The image shows a captured long-sleeve shirt segmented from its background. Further details are confidential.

Technical approach

Garments are captured with a camera and measured automatically using computer vision and camera calibration.

Engineering considerations

  • Accurate camera calibration so image positions map to real-world sizes.
  • Handling different garment styles, fabrics, and colours.
  • Keeping the workflow quick for operators.
